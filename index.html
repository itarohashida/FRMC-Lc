How to be a member of BMSS

Steps to become a BMSS member

1

Fill out the membership form

Complete the online BMSS membership form.

Open the membership form
2

Send 300 Tk via bKash

Use Send Money to 01991300531.
Write “BMSS Membership” in the reference.

3

Copy your TrxID

Copy the TrxID from your bKash payment and paste it into the form.

4

Submit for verification

Submit the form. Your payment will be verified.

A confirmation email will follow after verification.
